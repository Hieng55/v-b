# Mobile header and Home hero refinement

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation from the client on November 14, 2026.

## Brief

1. Vibe: refined, modern, bright, app-like, high-end. The client explicitly requires a white mobile background rather than midnight blue.
2. Journey in scope: recognize the V&B brand, see the nail artistry clearly, understand the promise, then book or explore services.
3. Energy curve: calm header, visual confidence in the photograph, quiet clarity in the ivory copy sheet, decisive gold booking action.
4. Desired feeling: calm first, cared for second, confident enough to book last. The memorable moment is the salon imagery resolving into a clean white action surface with booking always clear.
5. Signature move: on mobile only, navigation, slider controls and booking action behave like one coherent bright luxury app shell around a cinematic image stage.
6. Aesthetic range: premium-minimal application interface with cinematic photography, not a magazine layout and not a generic card dashboard.
7. Structure: distinct visual planes within one short opening scene, not a continuous cinematic world.
8. Existing assets: V&B logo, three approved generated Hero photographs, Jost and Forum, gold palette, salon contact details.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Role | Mobile behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photograph | Artistry and atmosphere | Occupies the upper frame with a portrait crop and slow restrained drift |
| Local image shade | Control contrast only | A narrow bottom band under slider controls, never a full-frame overlay |
| White application surface | Copy surface and foreground | Continues from the image as a flat app surface, without a floating editorial card |
| Typography | Brand promise | Espresso text centered on white, two lines, stable and fully readable |
| Controls | Slider agency | All slider controls share one compact app-style control capsule |

## Feeling curve and peak

- Opening: calm recognition from a compact white app bar.
- Image: desire from a brighter, less-obscured nail photograph.
- App surface: trust from centered hierarchy, controlled white space and consistent controls.
- Action: confidence from one gold booking button and a quieter Services link.

Peak: “The photography and controls feel like one polished luxury service app, and booking is immediately clear.”

Tell-someone sentence: It is the nail salon site that feels like a private luxury booking app.

## Boundaries

- This refinement changes only the mobile header and Home hero.
- Desktop composition and later sections remain unchanged.
- No visible text is smaller than 12px.
- Reduced motion keeps the complete static composition.
